`RESULTS.md` dated 2026-10-28; the summary and verdict live there, the complete numbers live here.

against. A model carrying no target information gives a margin of zero in expectation.

## Reading

- §C and §G answer the same question at two sample sizes (18 vs 1,386 pairs) and agree.
- §F shows the null is not an empty experiment: the candidates are geometrically distinct and
  their measured errors spread by 3× the margin being tested. The variation is real, it is just
  not target-specific.
- §D and §E are secondary: guidance strength trades spectrum push against passivity, and no
  single strength is a reliable default.
